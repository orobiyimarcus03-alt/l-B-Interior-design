When is my order confirmed?

Your order is received as pending. The store will contact you to confirm availability and delivery before processing it.

What if an item is damaged or incorrect?

What happens to my checkout details?

Your name, phone number and delivery address are saved with the order and visible to the store administrator for order fulfilment. The administrator can choose to open your delivery address in Google Maps; your address is sent to Google only if that link is opened. Do not include unnecessary sensitive information.
