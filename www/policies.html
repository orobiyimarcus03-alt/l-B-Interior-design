CUSTOMER INFORMATION

Store policies

Please review how orders are handled before placing an order.

Delivery & payment

Orders submitted through this website are requests and remain pending until the store confirms product availability and delivery arrangements. Any delivery charge, delivery timing and accepted payment method must be confirmed by the store before the order is processed.

This website does not currently collect online payments. Do not send payment until you have confirmed the order and payment details directly with the store.

Store contact details are not configured yet.

Returns & order issues

If an item is damaged, incorrect or missing, keep the item and packaging and contact the store with your order number and details of the issue. The store will review the order and explain the next steps.

Return eligibility and resolution may depend on the condition of the goods and applicable consumer-protection requirements. Contact the store before returning an item.

Privacy

When you place an order, your name, phone number, optional email address, delivery address and selected products are saved with the order. The store administrator can access these details to handle the order and customer support. For delivery assistance, the administrator may open the submitted address in Google Maps. The address is sent to Google only when the administrator clicks the map link. If you create an account, your name, email, phone number and a salted password hash are saved so you can sign in and view orders placed while signed in.

Do not submit payment-card details or other sensitive information through this site. This version does not verify account emails, provide email password resets, send automated email receipts, or process online payments. Order and account records are stored by the store operator; contact the store to ask about your information.

Ordering terms

Product prices and stock are shown as provided by the store and are checked again when an order is submitted. Submitting an order does not guarantee availability or create a confirmed delivery booking. The store will contact you before processing the order.

Product images, where provided, are illustrative. Product appearance, packaging and finish may vary.
